What to sanity-check

Is the scale recognizable? Do the acceleration bends feel plausible? Are the corners or direction changes objectionably clicky? Does the sound feel too tonal or too clean compared with your printer? Timestamps and which version you heard will make feedback useful.

The scale uses an assumed 8 acoustic cycles/mm for A/B—not measured steps/mm. Motion uses a P1S-reference approximation, without stock noise cancellation, measured enclosure resonances, or pressure advance. These clips do not establish whether a real P1S can play this scale.
