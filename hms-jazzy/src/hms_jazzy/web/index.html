SYMMETRIC PAIR
Symmetric motor mounting

Negative = aft, positive = forward. Edits are a draft; faded circles are active mounts. Apply resets pose, sea settings, and controls. Housing drag follows the mounts and can turn the boat in a cross-current. Inertia and bracket drag are not recomputed.

Wave conditions

IDEALIZED CHOP
Wave conditions

Adds a smaller crossing wave train. Height is not significant wave height. Water motion drives heave, roll, pitch, and drift; exposed propellers lose thrust. Changes apply immediately and may cause a transient.

No height, period, or steepness caps. Extreme waves or periods near/below the physics timestep can be inaccurate or unstable; these are not validated sea conditions.

Awaiting sea state

At the default 10 lbf per motor, provisional drag targets ~1.7 kn in calm water; a 2 kn head current carries the board backward. Increasing motor strength changes that balance. This is not a calibrated sea-keeping model.
